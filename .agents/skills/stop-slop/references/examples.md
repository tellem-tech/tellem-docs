# Editing examples

| Before | After | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| It is important to note that you should click Save to save your changes. | Click **Save**. | Remove the announcement and repetition. |
| In order to leverage this powerful feature, navigate to the delivery settings. | Open **Settings → Delivery**. | Use the interface path and a concrete verb. |
| На данный момент вы можете воспользоваться возможностью создания промокода. | Создайте промокод. | Remove a temporary qualifier and filler. |
| Возврат заказа не означает возврат денег. Это отдельный процесс. | Если нужно вернуть деньги, оформите возврат отдельно от возврата заказа. | Preserve the condition without inventing a provider workflow. |

Keep a condition when it changes the action: “If the store has added contacts, open **Profile → Support**” must retain that condition. Do not replace an uncertain result with a promise: “The provider accepted the message” does not mean “The customer received it.”
