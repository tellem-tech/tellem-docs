# Concise editing

Apply the repository's [content rules](../../../AGENTS.md), then edit for the reader's task.

- Lead with the action or fact. Cut announcements, rhetorical questions, promotional claims, and reassurance that adds no information.
- Replace jargon and vague adjectives with a concrete action, condition, or result. Remove words only when their removal preserves meaning.
- Give each fact one home. Link to the explanation from related tasks instead of repeating it.
- Prefer active verbs and short paragraphs. Use steps for a sequence, bullets for a set, and tables for comparisons.
- Match interface labels and product names. Keep Russian and English instructions equivalent.

Preserve prerequisites, exceptions, warnings, uncertainty, and legal meaning. System behavior can have a system subject; do not invent a human actor. Adverbs, passive voice, punctuation, and list length are editorial choices, not reasons to remove useful information.

Read the result as a complete procedure: can the reader find the action, meet its conditions, and understand the outcome? See [examples](references/examples.md) for typical cuts.
